Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Chartwise works fully offline with no account.

I found a wrong answer. Tap the report icon on the question. It opens an email draft with the question ID. We check every report against the cited regulation.

Can I move my progress to a new phone? Not in v1.0 (progress stays on the device; no sync).

Are these the real test questions? No. The FAA does not release live test questions. Chart figures are from the public FAA testing supplement; questions are original and written against the FAA Airman Certification Standards.
