Chartwise Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026.

Chartwise is a study app for the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot knowledge test. It collects no data.

  • No account, no sign-in, no sign-up.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests. All questions, charts and explanations are bundled with the app and work offline.
  • Your answers, mock tests, flags, settings and test date stay on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. Settings → Reset all progress also deletes them.
  • If you turn on the optional daily reminder, iOS asks permission for a local notification. It is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • “Report this question” opens a pre-filled email draft in your Mail app. It is sent only if you press Send, and contains the question ID and app version.

Chartwise is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Aviation Administration.
